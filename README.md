# PrintBuild 🏠

**Free tool that helps families visualize and price their home before construction starts.**

## What it does
- Generates 2D and 3D house plans based on your preferences
- Gives price estimates before construction begins
- Shows 10 variations per input
- Climate based recommendations
- Multiple roof types, elevations and themes

## Who it's for
Families planning to build a home who want clarity on design and cost before hiring an architect or contractor.
