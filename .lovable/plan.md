# Realistic Houses and Elevations

## Goal
Make every generated house read as a believable, buildable residence rather than a collection of simple 3D blocks, while preserving a distinct style and elevation for every variation.

## Exterior architecture
- Refine facade composition with consistent floor datum lines, recessed openings, real window/door reveals, sill and lintel depth, slab edges, plinths, shaded entrance portals, and structurally plausible balcony supports.
- Give each elevation family its own restrained architectural language: different roof silhouette, opening rhythm, material placement, balcony treatment, shading device, and landscape character.
- Keep decorative elements away from entrances, glazing, circulation, and roof drainage; align every upper-floor feature to its actual floor.
- Improve roof construction with fascia, eaves, ridges, gutters/downpipes, parapet coping, and sealed gable/butterfly ends.

## Realistic materials
- Upgrade the existing procedural finishes into coordinated albedo, roughness, and subtle relief maps for plaster, stone, timber, concrete, paving, grass, and roofs.
- Keep walls mostly warm off-white, then use timber, stone, brick, or metal as controlled accents rather than covering the entire house.
- Improve glazing with neutral clear glass, realistic reflections, dark or theme-matched frames, inset panes, and convincing interior depth behind windows.

## Site and presentation
- Replace the runtime environment preset with a reliable local studio-sky setup, physically balanced sun/sky lighting, tighter soft shadows, contact shading, and exposure tuned to avoid blown-out walls.
- Upgrade the site with a defined driveway, entrance path, kerbs, planting beds, varied shrubs, improved moving trees, and small grounding details around the plinth.
- Improve the default camera framing so the front elevation and one side are immediately readable at a natural architectural-photography angle.

## Variation quality
- Expand the design DNA mapping so named facade, window, roof, signature, landscape, and mood choices actually change the rendered model.
- Use deterministic variation rules so all ten models remain visibly different without random visual clutter.
- Keep geometry tied to the generated plan, balcony setting, openings, and floor structure so realism does not break usability.

## Validation
- Inspect representative flat, sloped, butterfly, courtyard, and multi-floor houses in the live viewer.
- Check all-floor and floor-by-floor views, balcony openings, roof closure, shadow quality, mobile framing, and walkthrough visibility.
- Confirm the preview builds cleanly and renders without missing assets or runtime errors.
